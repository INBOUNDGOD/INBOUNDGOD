Ultimatrix v2

Just the time, the date and your phone's battery. Flip the switch to see Always-On mode: everything fades away except the clock, the Ultimatrix hourglass and a ticking seconds mark.

Showing: wrist up

Phone battery

The only readout. It shows your phone's battery %, which the watch gets from the phone through the Galaxy Wearable app. The value shown is a sample.

Always-On mode

  • Stays: the time (as a green outline), the hourglass, and one green seconds mark ticking round the edge.
  • Goes: the date, the phone battery, the full seconds ring, the bezel and the glow.
  • To test on your watch: many watch faces only redraw once a minute in Always-On mode. Some still manage ticking seconds, so we'll check this one on your Watch4. If the mark freezes there, I'll tell you the options.
  • Only one small mark is lit, so the screen stays almost black, which saves battery.
