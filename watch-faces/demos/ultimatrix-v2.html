Ultimatrix v2

Phone battery now sits in the middle, between the two customizable slots. Flip the switch to see Always-On mode: everything fades away except the clock and the Ultimatrix hourglass.

Showing: wrist up

Bottom row

  • Left and right: customizable slots (Samsung calls them complications). They default to weather and heart rate here. Change either on the watch: touch and hold the face, then Customize.
  • Middle: your phone's battery %, read from the phone through the Galaxy Wearable app. The value shown is a sample.

Always-On mode

  • Stays: the time (as a green outline), the hourglass, and four small Ultimatrix notches at the edge.
  • Goes: the date, all three readouts, the seconds ring, the bezel and the glow.
  • The watch redraws only once a minute in Always-On mode, so the clock counts minutes there. The seconds ring runs whenever you raise your wrist.
  • Keeping Always-On this dark also saves battery. Samsung asks for most of the screen to stay black in that mode.
